Returning shopper

Rina

  • Mobile-first
  • Price-comparing
  • Low patience for forms
  • Loyal when it works

Rina buys from the store every few weeks, almost always on her phone during a commute or a break. She knows what she wants before she lands, so every extra step between the cart and the confirmation feels like friction. If checkout stalls, she leaves and finishes the purchase somewhere else.

O Illustrative

Support agent

Omar

  • Customer advocate
  • Pattern spotter
  • Works across tools
  • Process-minded

Omar answers chat and email for the store and hears about failed checkouts after the fact. He pieces together what went wrong from the customer's description, the order system, and whatever the payment provider logged. He is often the first person to notice when the same checkout problem keeps coming back.

Research methodology

7Participants
5Shoppers
2Support agents

Two personas built from seven synthetic interviews with shoppers and support agents about checkout drop-off. The returning shopper represents the customer side of the journey, while the support agent represents what happens after checkout fails.

Additional caveats

Persona status

  • Rina (Returning shopper) is a finding, supported by 4 participants (P01, P02, P03, P05).
  • Omar (Support agent) is a hypothesis, supported by 2 participants (P06, P07). That is below the 3-participant threshold, so treat it as directional, not settled.
  • P04 was a first-time shopper who fits neither persona and is not counted toward either.

Illustrative details

Names, portraits, gender, age, location, education, job, work experience and work setup are invented for readability. None of it describes a real participant.

Quality gate

ux-research-judge

A separate subagent, uncontaminated by the drafting context. Defined in .claude/agents/, scores against rubrics/.

draft → judge → revise → re-judge ↺ at most 2 → accept, or escalate to you

  • Per-criterion PASS / WARN / FAIL + ranked fixes. Warn-level clears the gate, any FAIL blocks it
  • Bias & leading-language screen, quote fidelity, evidence proportionality
  • Participant-count tracing. Every counted participant needs a displayed quote, a code-ID citation, or an adjacency note, verified one participant at a time with no sampling
  • Bounded retry. At most 2 re-judges, every pass logged in the artifact, then escalation to the human rather than a loop that passes by luck

Proven, not assumed

Regression guard

evals/, meta-evaluating the judge evals/run-evals.sh

Synthetic fixtures with planted defects (a leading discussion guide, an over-claimed synthesis, a counting-family fixture with untraced participants and compound claims) plus clean counterparts. The harness spawns the judge and asserts it flags the bad and passes the good, so tuning a rubric can't silently break the gate. A consistency case judges the same fixture twice and requires matching verdict, score, and untraced-participant count. If judge depth varies between passes, scores across revisions can't be compared.

Field-tested

Learned in live use

Hardened by the first real study evals/observed-failure-modes.md

The first study ran 11 participants through 3 judge passes. Every correction concerned participant counting and claim scoping, so the synthesis rubric, the skills, and the eval suite were rebalanced toward the errors that occur in practice. Quote fidelity held clean, with zero fabrications across roughly 40 checked quotes. Each judge pass is logged in evals/run-log.md, and the remaining follow-ups live in Open items.

Growing edges

New templates & skills

A brand-neutral journey-layers visualization extracted from the study, templates/journey-layers.html, with stages, a tool matrix, roles, and sentiment wired to evidence. It was the deliverable stakeholders responded to most strongly in live use, and a filled-in synthetic demo shows the full effect. A personas overview, templates/personas-overview.html, is now the standard design for every persona deliverable, with a synthetic demo. A Product Brief review skill in Product-brief-SKILL.md. A computed-styles visual baseline in docs/design-baseline.md so HTML deliverables reuse one verified palette on every study.

The value is in evaluating better. AI drafts and organizes. Human judgment is the product.
